Privacy Policy

“National Competency Exam Simulator” App | Kafa'a

Last Updated: February 21, 2026

Important Notice: This application is an independent educational tool and does not represent any governmental body. For official information and instructions regarding the national exam, please visit the official website of the Iraqi Ministry of Higher Education and Scientific Research.

At the development team of the “National Competency Exam Simulator” app, we place the utmost importance on the privacy of our users — students and educators alike. This Privacy Policy explains what information and data we collect, how we use it, and with whom we may share it, to provide a safe and transparent educational experience.

By using the “Kafa'a” app, you agree to the data collection and usage practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect certain data necessary to provide, personalize, and improve our services to you, including:

a. Account Information (Personal Data)

When you register in the app — either by creating an account with an email address or by signing in with a Google account — we collect and store your full name, email address, phone number (if provided), and profile photo (if available in your Google account).

b. Usage & Performance Data (Test Results)

We store and record your scores across all in-app exams (English, Arabic, and Computer Science). This includes the number of correct and incorrect answers, topics requiring improvement, and time spent answering questions.

c. Technical Data (Device & App Data)

We may automatically collect anonymized technical information, including IP address, operating system type, app version, Advertising IDs, and app interaction data; this is used to monitor performance and diagnose technical issues.

d. User-Generated Content (Success Stories)

When sharing your experience on the “Honor Board,” we collect the advice you write, your academic specialty, the selected subject, and any certificate images you choose to upload from your device to document your success.

2. How We Use Your Information

We do not sell your personal data to any third party. The data we collect is used for the following purposes:

  • Securely managing your personal account and enabling continuous access to the national exam simulation, including syncing your data across multiple devices.
  • Analyzing your academic level and performance to provide personalized statistics such as pass rate, best score, cumulative topic performance, and strengths and weaknesses.
  • Enabling result sharing and printing, should you wish to share your score with your teachers or peers.
  • Monitoring app performance, analyzing errors, and making improvements.
  • Public display on the Honor Board: information you submit (name, score, advice, and certificate image) will be visible to all app users after review and approval by the administration, for the purpose of motivating students.

3. Advertising & Third-Party Services

To support the continued operation and development of the “Kafa'a” app, we integrate third-party advertising and infrastructure services, each subject to its own privacy practices:

  • Ad Networks (Google AdMob & Meta Audience Network): The app displays integrated ads. These companies use technologies such as cookies and Advertising IDs to deliver ads tailored to your interests.
  • Infrastructure & Authentication Services (Google Firebase): We use the Firebase platform to secure registration and store your data in a cloud environment with encryption safeguards.

4. Data Security & Protection

We implement security measures, including encryption via secure protocols, to protect your personal data and statistics from unauthorized access or breach. Your data is stored on cloud servers operated by Google.

5. Location & Digital Privacy

The app does not collect or request access to your precise geographic location (GPS). Technical information collected, such as IP addresses, is used for general statistical purposes and to help deliver educational content appropriate to the Iraqi national curriculum.

6. Data Retention Period

We retain your test results and personal information for as long as your account remains active, enabling you to track your educational journey and progress. If you wish to stop using the app, you may request deletion as described below.

7. User Rights & Data Control

  • Edit Your Data: You may update your personal information through the “Profile” screen within the app.
  • Delete Account & Data: The “Profile” section provides a delete button to permanently erase your account and associated statistics.
  • External Deletion Request: If you uninstall the app and wish to have your data removed from our servers, send a deletion request to mzuory@gmail.com.

8. Children's Privacy

This app is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect identifying data from this age group. If we discover that a child's data under this age has been inadvertently collected, we will delete it without delay.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy from time to time to reflect technical, legal, or service-related changes. We will notify users of material changes via an in-app notification before the changes take effect.

10. User-Generated Content Policy (UGC) — Honor Board

  • Pre-Publication Review: All submitted stories and images are subject to manual review by the administration team before appearing publicly to ensure content appropriateness.
  • Reporting & Blocking Tools: Users have the option to “Report” inappropriate content and “Block” another user. Reports will be addressed within 24 hours of receipt.

11. Access Permissions

The app requests access to media (Gallery/Photos) for the specific purpose of allowing you to select a certificate image to upload and share as part of your success story on the Honor Board. We do not access any other photos or private files on your device.

12. Contact Us

For inquiries or requests related to this Privacy Policy or your personal data, contact mzuory@gmail.com.
